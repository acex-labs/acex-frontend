ZTP > Approvals lists discoveries under the tabs To review, Approved, Rejected and All. Opening one shows what approving would do to the asset (create, update, no change or blocked) and any conflicts; you can approve or reject it, or select several and approve or reject them in one step after a preview. Approving does not push any configuration.
