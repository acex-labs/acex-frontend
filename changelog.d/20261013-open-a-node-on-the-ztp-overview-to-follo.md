title: Open a node on the ZTP Overview to follow it and act on it
refs: #17
---
Clicking a node opens a side panel with its progress (asset claimed, device discovered, identity approved, provisioning, provisioned), its discoveries and its provisioning jobs. From there you can Start ZTP, Provision, go Back to ready, Cancel ZTP or Unclaim the node; most of these ask for a second click first.
