Every line on the About page now starts with the build of its component, written like its image tag (`dev-` and the commit for development builds) and linking to the commit, followed by the version, and a link shows what has landed on dev since. A new Agents section lists the collection and telemetry agents with their status and, once they report it, the version and build they run.
