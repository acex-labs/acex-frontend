kind: added
title: Sites show compliance on the Overview and per node on a Compliance tab
---
The site Overview has a Compliance card under the map: a ring with how much of the site's desired configuration the devices match, and how many nodes are below 90 %. The new Compliance tab adds the site's totals, including undeclared components and unparsed lines, and a bar per node, worst first. Click a node to open its configuration.
