title: Software compliance has a page of the assets and their versions
---
Compliance > Software lists every asset on a node with its software status: not allowed, unknown version, no policy, accepted or golden. Each row shows the node, site, model, serial number, the version the asset runs and the golden version to aim for. Pick one or more statuses with the buttons above the list, each with how many assets it would show, and narrow it further by site or region or with the search field, which looks through node, site, serial number, model, version and cluster. Sort by any of the main columns. Click an asset to open it, or its node name to open the node. The choices are kept in the address, so a view can be shared. Details on the Software compliance cards on the dashboard and on the compliance overview now open this page. The software policy is one click away at the top right.
