---
kind: added
title: Regions have a page of their own, with their compliance and sites
---
A region name now opens a page that shows how the region looks. Click it in the compliance overview, in the Regions list or in a search result.

The page counts the region's sites, node instances, logical nodes and assets; each count opens that list already filtered to the region. It shows the region's configuration and software compliance, a table of its sites with the one furthest from compliance first (a site name opens that site's Compliance tab), and a map zoomed to the sites. Edit and delete are in the page header.

The Logical Nodes list has a Region filter, next to the ones on Nodes and Sites.
