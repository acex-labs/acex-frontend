kind: fixed
title: Site names in the compliance rankings open the site's compliance details
---
On the Compliance overview, the site names in the top and worst site lists looked like plain text and opened the site's Overview tab. They are now shown as links, and a click opens the Compliance tab of the site, scrolled to the configuration or the software section, depending on the list. There the site's nodes are listed, the ones furthest from compliance first.
